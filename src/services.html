MORE THAN BOOKS

Useful services, all in one place.

Make the most of your library visit with our in-person and digital facilities.

🔎

Online catalogue

Search titles and check availability using the library catalogue terminal during opening hours, Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–8 PM.

📱

E-books

Registered members can request access to the digital reading collection at the help desk. Access is available every day.

🪑

Study-room booking

Reserve a study room at the front desk at least one day in advance. Rooms are available 10 AM–7 PM, subject to availability.

🖨️

Printing and scanning

Bring your files on a USB drive or use the library computer. Available Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–7:30 PM. Charges apply.

📶

Free Wi-Fi

Members and visitors can request the daily Wi-Fi access details from reception during library opening hours.
